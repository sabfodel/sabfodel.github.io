Slow going, but I don't regret it. Rather than stating a formula and moving on, the writing
circles the question of *why it has to be this way* — the chapters on where the free electron
model falls apart are especially good at this.

**So far:** chapters 1–13. I pulled the Bloch theorem and band structure material out into
[[bloch-theorem]].

**Hardest part:** semiclassical dynamics. The effective mass tensor appears looking like it
came from nowhere and it took me a while to sit comfortably with it.

**Worth knowing:** it's a 1976 book and it shows — there is nothing here about topological
phases. You'll need another source for that.
