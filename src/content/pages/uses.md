## Desk

*(Photo goes here — drop the file into `public/img/` and embed it.)*

- **Desk** — nothing special, but deep enough that the violin case has somewhere to live
- **Chair** — the single thing I'd tell anyone to spend money on. Six-hour problem sets are
  a posture problem before they're a physics problem.
- **Lamp** — warm, dimmable, aimed at the wall rather than the page

## Machine

- **Laptop** — Windows, with most of the real work happening in a terminal
- **Keyboard** — mechanical, tactile switches, no numpad
- **Second monitor** — paper on one side, editor on the other. Hard to give up once you've
  had it.

## Violin

- **Violin** — a student instrument, and honestly well past what my playing deserves
- **Bow** — the shop's recommendation, which I'm in no position to second-guess
- **Shoulder rest** — took three tries to find one that didn't fight me
- **Tuner and drone app** — the drone is the important half. See [[violin-first-months]].

## Software

- **[Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/)** — every note I take, including the ones on this site
- **Doom Emacs** — code, and anything that needs serious text surgery
- **Rust toolchain** — see [[qsim-rs]] and [[gargantua-rs]]
- **Astro** — what this site is built with
- **Git** — including for these notes, which turns out to be the best habit I picked up
  this year

## Notes system

Zettelkasten, loosely. One idea per file, linked heavily, filenames in English so the links
stay stable. The physics notes on this site are the ones that survived being cleaned up —
the raw vault is considerably messier, and stays private.
