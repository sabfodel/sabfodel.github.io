I've kept my notes in Obsidian for a while now. It works — but it's a closed box. I only
ever find out whether I actually understand something when I try to explain it to someone,
and explaining it to someone requires there to be a somewhere.

This is the somewhere.

## What goes here

- **Notes.** My study notes, written up properly. Derivations, intuition, and the "why did
  we take that step" questions.
- **Projects.** The code I write and — more to the point — what I learned writing it.
- **Journal.** Things like this.

## What doesn't

No ads, no cookies, no tracking, no comment box. If you want to say something there's
[email](/about/). There's an RSS feed too, because I don't need an algorithm deciding what
I read.

The whole thing is written by hand. Dark, red, and a deliberate nod to the personal pages of
the early 2000s. I'd missed marquees.

## Right now

I'm working through quantum error correction. The stabiliser formalism looks like a
bookkeeping trick at first glance, but the further in I get the more I suspect that's where
the actual story is. There's [a note on the toric code](/notes/toric-code/) up already.

See you around.
